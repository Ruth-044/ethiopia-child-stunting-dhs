# ethiopia-child-stunting-dhs
Factors associated with stunting in Ethiopian children under 5 (DHS 2024–25), and prediction across regions and wealth groups
# Research question

Among children under 5 in Ethiopia (DHS 2024–25):

1. Which child, maternal and household factors are most strongly associated with stunting, and how does this differ by region?
2. Do these relationships make biological and clinical sense according to pediatric evidence (e.g. Nelson Textbook of Pediatrics), or are they only statistical patterns?
3. Can these factors be used to predict which children are stunted?
4. Does that prediction work equally well across regions and wealth groups?

# Data

Data come from the Demographic and Health Surveys (DHS) Program. The raw files cannot be shared, so they are not in this repository. You can request free access at https://dhsprogram.com.
